<Section n="01" title="Notation" id="notation">

<dl class="note-defs">
  <dt>$n$</dt><dd>number of input features</dd>
  <dt>$m$</dt><dd>number of training examples</dd>
  <dt>$(i)$</dt><dd>refers to the $i$-th training example</dd>
  <dt>$[l]$</dt><dd>refers to the $l$-th layer of the network</dd>
  <dt>$L$</dt><dd>the output layer, and the number of layers: hidden layers plus the output layer</dd>
  <dt>$\{x^{(i)}, y^{(i)}\}$</dt><dd>the $i$-th training example: input features $x$ and labelled output $y$</dd>
  <dt>$\hat y$ or $a$</dt><dd>the prediction: for the same $x$ we want $\hat y \approx y$. Also written $a$, for activation</dd>
  <dt>$g$</dt><dd>activation function</dd>
  <dt>$\{W, b\}$</dt><dd>the model: parameters learned during training</dd>
  <dt>$\alpha$</dt><dd>learning rate</dd>
  <dt>$\lambda$</dt><dd>regularisation parameter</dd>
</dl>

<Section n="02" title="Neuron and activation" id="neuron">

For one training example, vectorised over all the units of layer $l$. The index $(i)$ is left out.

<Key label="Linear input">

$$
z^{[l]} = W^{[l]} \cdot a^{[l-1]} + b^{[l]} \qquad \in \R^{n^{[l]} \times 1}
$$

</Key>

<Key label="Activation, the non-linear output">

$$
a^{[l]} = g^{[l]}\big(z^{[l]}\big) \qquad \in \R^{n^{[l]} \times 1}
$$

</Key>

Four usual choices for $g$. The dashed curve is the derivative $g'$, which backward propagation needs.

</Section>

<Section n="03" title="Prediction" id="prediction">

The output layer gives the prediction. It is computed by **forward propagation**.

<Key label="Prediction">

$$
\hat y^{(i)} = a^{[L](i)} \qquad \in \R^{n^{[L]} \times 1}
$$

</Key>

</Section>

<Section n="04" title="Vectorisation" id="vectorisation">

Put the $m$ training examples side by side as columns, and one pass computes the whole training set.

<div class="note-grid">

$$
X = \begin{bmatrix}
\mid & \mid & & \mid \\
x^{(1)} & x^{(2)} & \cdots & x^{(m)} \\
\mid & \mid & & \mid
\end{bmatrix} \in \R^{n \times m}
$$

$$
Y = \begin{bmatrix}
\mid & \mid & & \mid \\
y^{(1)} & y^{(2)} & \cdots & y^{(m)} \\
\mid & \mid & & \mid
\end{bmatrix} \in \R^{n^{[L]} \times m}
$$

$$
Z^{[l]} = \begin{bmatrix}
\mid & \mid & & \mid \\
z^{[l](1)} & z^{[l](2)} & \cdots & z^{[l](m)} \\
\mid & \mid & & \mid
\end{bmatrix} \in \R^{n^{[l]} \times m}
$$

$$
A^{[l]} = \begin{bmatrix}
\mid & \mid & & \mid \\
a^{[l](1)} & a^{[l](2)} & \cdots & a^{[l](m)} \\
\mid & \mid & & \mid
\end{bmatrix} \in \R^{n^{[l]} \times m}
$$

$$
B^{[l]} = \begin{bmatrix}
\mid & \mid & & \mid \\
b^{[l]} & b^{[l]} & \cdots & b^{[l]} \\
\mid & \mid & & \mid
\end{bmatrix} \in \R^{n^{[l]} \times m}
$$

</div>

<Key label="For each layer">

$$
\begin{aligned}
Z^{[l]} &= W^{[l]} \cdot A^{[l-1]} + B^{[l]} \\[4pt]
A^{[l]} &= g^{[l]}\big(Z^{[l]}\big)
\end{aligned}
$$

<p class="note-key-note">with $Z^{[l]}, A^{[l]} \in \R^{n^{[l]} \times m}$</p>

</Key>

</Section>

<Section n="05" title="Forward propagation" id="forward">

<Key label="Algorithm">

$$
\begin{aligned}
& A^{[0]} = X \\[2pt]
& \textbf{for } l = 1 \textbf{ to } L \\
& \qquad Z^{[l]} = W^{[l]} \cdot A^{[l-1]} + B^{[l]} \\
& \qquad A^{[l]} = g^{[l]}\big(Z^{[l]}\big)
\end{aligned}
$$

</Key>

The last activation, $A^{[L]}$, is the prediction for all the training examples.

</Section>

<Section n="06" title="Cost function" id="cost">

The cost is the average of the loss $\mathcal{L}$ over the $m$ training examples.

<Key label="Cost">

$$
J = \frac{1}{m} \sum_{i=1}^{m} \mathcal{L}\big(a^{[L](i)}, y^{(i)}\big)
$$

<p class="note-key-note">$J$ is a function of all the parameters $W^{[1]}, b^{[1]}, \ldots, W^{[L]}, b^{[L]}$</p>

</Key>

With the cross-entropy loss, for a binary output:

$$
\begin{aligned}
J = -\frac{1}{m} \sum_{i=1}^{m} \Big[\, & y^{(i)} \log a^{[L](i)} \\
+ & \big(1 - y^{(i)}\big) \log\big(1 - a^{[L](i)}\big) \Big]
\end{aligned}
$$

</Section>

<Section n="07" title="Backward propagation" id="backward">

Notation: for any variable $v$ of the loss, $dv$ is the partial derivative of the loss with respect to $v$. For example:

$$
dv = \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial v}
\qquad\qquad
dz^{[l]} = \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial z^{[l]}}
$$

<Key label="Algorithm">

$$
\begin{aligned}
& dA^{[L]} = \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial A^{[L]}}\big(A^{[L]}, Y\big) \\[4pt]
& \textbf{for } l = L \textbf{ down to } 1 \\
& \qquad dZ^{[l]} = dA^{[l]} \odot g^{[l]\prime}\big(Z^{[l]}\big) \\[2pt]
& \qquad dW^{[l]} = \frac{1}{m}\, dZ^{[l]} \cdot A^{[l-1]T} \\[2pt]
& \qquad dB^{[l]} = \frac{1}{m} \sum_{i=1}^{m} dZ^{[l](i)} \\[2pt]
& \qquad dA^{[l-1]} = W^{[l]T} \cdot dZ^{[l]}
\end{aligned}
$$

</Key>

$\odot$ is the element-wise product. The sum for $dB$ runs over the columns, one per training example.

### Why: the chain rule

For one example, with $z^{[l]} = W^{[l]} a^{[l-1]} + b^{[l]}$ and $a^{[l]} = g^{[l]}(z^{[l]})$:

<div class="note-derive">

$$
\begin{alignedat}{2}
dz^{[l]} &= \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial a^{[l]}} \cdot \frac{\partial a^{[l]}}{\partial z^{[l]}} &&= da^{[l]} \odot g^{[l]\prime}\big(z^{[l]}\big) \\[10pt]
da^{[l-1]} &= \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial z^{[l]}} \cdot \frac{\partial z^{[l]}}{\partial a^{[l-1]}} &&= W^{[l]T} \cdot dz^{[l]} \\[10pt]
dW^{[l]} &= \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial z^{[l]}} \cdot \frac{\partial z^{[l]}}{\partial W^{[l]}} &&= dz^{[l]} \cdot a^{[l-1]T} \\[10pt]
db^{[l]} &= \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial z^{[l]}} \cdot \frac{\partial z^{[l]}}{\partial b^{[l]}} &&= dz^{[l]}
\end{alignedat}
$$

</div>

</Section>

<Section n="08" title="Gradient descent" id="gradient-descent">

<Figure

  caption="Layer l in both passes. Forward caches Z, backward returns the gradients. Drawn with NodeBox 3."
  width={800}
  height={600}
  narrow
/>

<ol class="note-steps">
  <li class="note-step-init">
    <span class="note-step-n">0</span>
    <div><b>Initialise</b> the parameters $W^{[l]}$ and $B^{[l]}$ of every layer, the weights at random.</div>
  </li>
  <li class="note-loop">
    <span class="note-loop-label">Repeat until convergence</span>
    <ol>
      <li><span class="note-step-n">1</span><div><b>Forward propagation</b> from $X$ to the prediction $A^{[L]}$.</div></li>
      <li><span class="note-step-n">2</span><div><b>Cost</b> $J$, mainly to check that it decreases from one iteration to the next.</div></li>
      <li><span class="note-step-n">3</span><div><b>Start backward propagation</b> with $dA^{[L]} = \partial \mathcal{L} / \partial A^{[L]}$.</div></li>
      <li><span class="note-step-n">4</span><div><b>Backward propagation</b> down to layer 1, giving $dW^{[l]}$ and $dB^{[l]}$.</div></li>
      <li>
        <span class="note-step-n">5</span>
        <div>
          <b>Update</b> the parameters of each layer:

$$
\begin{aligned}
W^{[l]} &:= W^{[l]} - \alpha \, dW^{[l]} \\
B^{[l]} &:= B^{[l]} - \alpha \, dB^{[l]}
\end{aligned}
$$

        </div>
      </li>
    </ol>
  </li>
</ol>

</Section>
